# Progress review — Lesson 1

Recorded 2026-10-22, before extending the curriculum. Original full narrative: [progression report 019](https://github.com/AGI144348Outlook/dual-order-os/blob/experiment/echo-swadesh-fresh-start/research/progression/019-correction-learning-classroom.md).

Echo began with no taught label-to-selector associations in the separate lesson session. Four structural hypotheses were supplied: first entity, last entity, speaker and addressee. The teacher gave I/you examples, speaker changes and external feedback. No I/you answer rule was added to the predictor.

After 48 practice turns, the B1-c-inspired counter bridge improved from 1/8 to 8/8 on fresh entity IDs. The original A-101/A-113 chosen-arm-feedback setup improved from 1/8 to 2/8. The no-feedback control stayed at 1/8. The policies received different feedback; these figures do not establish algorithm superiority.

What went well: corrections changed subsequent selections; arbitrary/reversed label conventions could be learned; prediction remained frozen during assessment; pending and completed state restored; experience cards persisted; a mobile teacher could interrupt, point to a referent and provide a correction.

What was not established: the eight checks reused the same four structural scene templates. They did not establish novel-layout transfer, natural-language comprehension, visual perception, correct first-person ownership, a definition in qualified vocabulary, independent note composition or autonomous Recess. Mechanical copying and displayed entity names were supplied interface scaffolding.

Therefore the entries remained yellow. The next lesson should check retained associations with genuinely different arrangements and missing context before extending to interrogatives or granting broader qualification. The empty initial profile and historical evidence remain preserved.
